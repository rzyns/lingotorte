      Watch-session sketch

      Design artifact, not implementation. Synthetic scene and synthetic dictionary entries; nothing here is measured. Every behaviour below is a proposal unless marked exists (source-reading of the current app).

      Try it

      1. Click the video to pause/resume P-02. [ ] R exists.
      2. Pointer: hover płaszcz ≈300 ms → possible meanings, playback continues. Click it → kept, paused, toast with Undo. No form, no meaning asked.
      3. Hover a word and wait: the line advances but the popover stays attached to that line (amber "from the line at…") and the strip freezes P-04.
      4. Switch Input → Touch: tap a word = inspect + pause; Keep is inside the popover. Long-press a word, then tap neighbours, to build a phrase P-05.
      5. Pointer phrase: drag-select sobie poradzę (last line) → phrase lookup; Agnieszko → no dictionary match, Keep still works.
      6. Dictionary → Unavailable: lookup says so honestly; Keep still works. Saving → Fails: one shared indicator + "Couldn't save · Retry".
      7. Keyboard: Tab into the line, ←/→ between words (popover follows focus), Enter on the focused word keeps, Esc closes.

      Proposals (unresolved)

      • P-01 Strip, not overlay. Words live in a strip below the video so tap-video (pause) and tap-word (inspect) never share pixels. Cost: no word-level tap on burned-in-style overlay subtitles.
      • P-02 Click video = pause. Missing today.
      • P-03 Pointer splits inspect/collect by gesture (hover vs click); touch and keyboard split by one extra action inside the popover. Hover delay ≈300 ms is a guess.
      • P-04 Moving-line rule. Popover owns a copy of {line, span, text} at open; strip freezes while open; the popover says which line it belongs to. Alternative not sketched: let the strip advance and pin the popover.
      • P-05 Phrase on touch = long-press then tap to extend. Unverified on a real device.
      • P-06 Tap-to-inspect pauses on touch, hover never does. No timing-based auto-resume (invisible rule).
      • P-07 Senses are "possible meanings"; top sense is not written as the card's meaning. The kept item records text + line + moment only.
      • P-08 One shared save indicator for the whole queue; no per-verb "kept but unsaved".
      • P-09 Replay line is reachable from the popover — the owner's "get back to the start of the sentence" need, where the eye already is.

      Deliberately out of scope

      • Navigation shell, study session, correction ("Fix this line"), approval marker (Q-04), scheduling toggle (future option).
      • Whether "Choose what to study…" lives in this drawer or in Words — shown disabled as a placeholder.

      Accessibility intent

      • Words are role=button + arrow navigation (as today, lt-z8s); popover is a role=dialog that follows keyboard focus; toast and sync are live regions; focus returns to the word after Keep/close.
