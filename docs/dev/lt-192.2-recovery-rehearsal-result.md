## What the rehearsal proves [run]

1. **Seeded store through real save paths.** The seeded store contains:
   - two media (`m1`, `m2`), each with approved target and native tracks;
   - an approved corrected version of `m1`'s target (`trackVersion` 2, `manual-edit`, `provenance.parentTrackId` = v1), with word timings on one cue;
   - a sentence with a learner meaning;
   - a phrase with notes, whose card was suspended through the Saved-view operation (`archiveReviewCardsForSavedItem`);
   - a lexeme captured in both videos, with a dictionary-suggested meaning and notes, and an occurrence time range derived from the word timings (1400–1700 ms);
   - review events and FSRS-updating practice attempts.

   A *Replace all* restore of a learner-state manifest then removes a disposable reviewed and practised item, so its two review events and one attempt become **inactive, retained-only audit**. The same restore sets `archivedAt` on another item. This is the **store/restore archive fixture**, kept separate from the UI suspend case.
2. **Checkpoint.** A `node:sqlite` `backup()` of the live WAL database. The backup file is closed straight away and **never opened again**. Its SHA-256 is unchanged at the end, and no `-wal`/`-shm` siblings exist. The logical snapshot and table state come from disposable copies. The logical snapshot covers: schema version, applied migrations, audit recovery metadata, `rev:n`, the canonical learner state, audit rows, and the bundles of every track (original and corrected, cues and word timings). The **original** database, closed, has the same logical snapshot as the checkpoint.
3. **Browser at the exact baseline, plus offline additions.** A browser connects to the checkpoint service and hydrates **all** media. Its cache record is kept as the cache checkpoint. Assertions: it holds no cues, no pending tracks, and an acknowledgement baseline bound to that origin. The cache strips acknowledged bundles. The service is then closed. Offline, the browser adds:
   - a third media `m3`, whose bundles are pending in cache v2;
   - a lexeme with a learner meaning and a review event;
   - a phrase with notes on `m1`.

   **[fixture]** The fixture builder checks that the candidate is the checkpoint plus new ids only. A same-id payload conflict, or a baseline record missing from the candidate, is refused (negative cases asserted).
4. **Transfer (bundle-first, CAS).** Steps:
   - A working copy of the checkpoint is opened behind a new service.
   - A browser hydrates from the candidate cache and connects. Because the origin differs, the Keep/Load choice appears.
   - **[fixture]** The precondition confirms the service is still exactly at the checkpoint.
   - The browser chooses Keep browser and saves. The exact request order is `PUT /api/tracks/{m3 target|native}/transcript 200` ×2, then `PUT /api/state`, which commits.
5. **Lost acknowledgement, resolved by readback.** The `PUT /api/state` reply is consumed and the client receives a network error. The client reports failure (`lastSavedAt` null) and **does not retry**. The resolution:
   - **[current]** Reconnect raises the choice again. `connectLocalService` compares exact `JSON.stringify` serializations. The service returns content-identical learner state, but the records come back in a different order. A key-sorted comparison is equal; the raw serializations differ in `mediaAssets`, `subtitleTracks`, `savedItems`, `savedOccurrences` and `reviewCardStates`. So the product does not recognise its own committed write.
   - The test resolves it by readback. It asserts that the service learner state is content-identical to the browser's and that no bundle is pending, then chooses **Load service state**. No PUT is sent after the loss.
   - The final revision is exactly checkpoint + 3 (two bundles plus one learner-state commit). Item, review-event, track and bundle ids are exact, and no saved text is duplicated.
6. **Reopen and full rehydration.** The candidate service is closed and reopened on the same file, and the revision is unchanged. A fresh browser connects. Before hydration, only the current media is loaded, and the Anki plan *differs* from the expected plan, because the context falls back to the bare selection. After loading every media through `GET /api/media/{id}/transcripts`, the browser's full store equals the **independently combined expected state**: the checkpoint logical snapshot plus the recorded new-id records. The checks cover:
   - all ids and payloads;
   - `meaning`/`meaningSource`/`notes`/`archivedAt`;
   - the suspended card;
   - review and practice records and the audit;
   - track versions and parents;
   - cues and word timings;
   - every occurrence's `cueId`/`charSpan`, which resolves to a cue on its own track whose slice equals `selectionText`, with a matching media fingerprint.

   **Anki:** the checkpoint `ankiGuidFor` set is a subset of the transferred set. The difference is exactly the two added active items, and the archived item is excluded. `buildAnkiExportPlan` on the hydrated transferred store equals the plan built from the expected state. The closed candidate file's logical snapshot also equals the expected state.
7. **Stale comparison sentinel (explicit timing).** The order is fixed:
   1. The browser connects, and its pending choice captures `rev:n`.
   2. A second client then writes a sentinel meaning (`rev:n+1`).
   3. Only then does the browser choose Keep browser and save, with no reconnect in between.

   The browser's **first** PUT, the `m3` bundle, gets **409**. Sync goes to `conflict`. No `PUT /api/state` is sent, and the browser work is kept. The service is unchanged: the sentinel is present, `m3` media and bundles are absent, and the added items are absent. The event log asserts the order choice → sentinel → first browser PUT.
8. **Already-present divergence.** The sentinel is written *before* the browser reconnects, so the comparison revision already includes it. CAS cannot protect it from omission by replacement. **[fixture]** The rehearsal precondition detects the divergence and refuses; the test cancels the choice. Product Keep browser is **not** attempted, and **no PUT** is sent. The service state is unchanged and the browser work is kept. This is a fixture refusal, **not a product safeguard**. The product has none for this case; that is C (`lt-192.3`) territory. B implements no merge.
9. **Rollback (cache + DB).** The candidate copy is deleted. A fresh working copy of the immutable checkpoint opens behind a new service, on a fresh origin. The browser boots from the **recorded checkpoint cache record**, which is byte-identical to its recording. Results:
   - Connect adopts the service without a prompt.
   - After full rehydration, the store equals the checkpoint, and so do the Anki GUIDs and plan and the learner-export manifest content.
   - A candidate-era revision token gets **409** against the rolled-back store, which stays at `rev:n`.
   - After close, the logical snapshot equals the checkpoint, and the table-level state equals that of a never-serviced copy of the checkpoint (logical rows, not byte identity).
   - **Token reuse across lineages:** the next write on the rolled-back store gets the token the candidate used after its first bundle (`rev:n+1`), but it names a state without `m3`. Revisions are compared within one lineage only.

Optional §6.5 learner-only *Merge/update* restore: not run; the required scenarios did not need it. §6.6 (independent service changes plus new browser media) is not merged anywhere. Only its refusal or conflict is shown (items 7–8).

## Findings for later slices (not fixed here)

- **[current] Lost-ack reconnect raises a false choice.** After a committed-but-unacknowledged learner-state save, `connectLocalService` compares order-sensitive serializations, so it does not recognise the identical committed state. Resolving this needs a readback judgment by the user or caller. Choosing Keep browser instead would send a second, CAS-checked replacement of identical content (source-read, not run). C owns duplicate-safe retry and acknowledgement.
- **[current] Already-present divergence is unprotected.** A service change that is already present at comparison time is silently omitted if the user then chooses Keep browser. This is source-confirmed only; the rehearsal deliberately did not perform the unsafe save. C.
- **[current] Export depends on which media are loaded.** Without full rehydration, Anki contexts degrade silently to the bare selection (observed in item 6). G must handle this (A §2.6).
